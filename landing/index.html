01 — Why factory-built

Rain doesn’t stop
a factory.

Traditional builds lose months to weather, subcontractor gaps and a price that only settles at the end. We removed all three by moving the build indoors.

01

The quote is the price

Material costs are locked the day your order enters the line. If steel moves in month three, that is our problem — your contract doesn’t move.

02

Two builds running at once

While your foundation cures on site, your house is being wired 400 km away. Nine weeks of factory time costs you almost no calendar time.

03

Built to survive a crane

A hot-dip galvanised frame engineered for road transport and a lift is heavily over-specified for standing still on a plot. That’s the point.

Usually yes, and it depends entirely on your local authority — the module is a permanent building, not a caravan, which is normally a help rather than a hindrance. We supply stamped structural drawings, thermal calculations and a site plan as part of every order, which is the pack most authorities ask for. We do not submit the application for you, but we have never had a client refused on the grounds of the structure itself.

For One and Duo, galvanised screw piles — installed in a day, no concrete, no curing time, and reversible if you ever move the house. Loft ships as two modules and needs either piles with a steel grillage or a shallow reinforced slab, depending on ground conditions. We specify the foundation from your soil data and can arrange the crew, or work with yours.

The standard envelope is rated for −15 °C; the alpine package on Loft is rated to −25 °C and is a $4,100 option on the other two. Roofs are engineered to 1.6 kN/m² snow load as standard and can be uprated. Triple glazing is standard, not an upsell. Units are running year-round in Slovenia, Poland and northern Scotland.

Anywhere in the EU and the UK by road. The first 200 km from the factory is included; beyond that it is roughly $2.90 per km per module, plus ferry where relevant. The bigger question is usually your access — we need 3.6 m of width for the truck and a crane standing position within about 12 m of the final location. Send a photo of your approach and we will tell you honestly whether it works.

Within the shell, yes — window positions, partition walls, kitchen side, cladding colour and the bathroom spec are all open. The frame dimensions are not, because that is where the price and the nine weeks come from. If you need a genuinely bespoke footprint we will say so and point you to an architect, rather than sell you a compromise.

Three stages: 30% when the drawings are signed off and your slot is booked, 50% when the frame is complete and photographed, 20% on the day you accept handover. Nothing is due when you request a quote, and the deposit is refundable for 14 days. Financing through our partner lenders starts at 6.4% APR, subject to status.
