## Content rules

- One primary intent per page; do not create several pages that compete for the same keyword.
- Country pages must contain genuinely country-specific context, entities and integration examples.
- Service and industry pages support country pages through contextual internal links.
- Copy targets operational buyers, not developers. Lead with business constraints, outcomes, ownership, integration and risk reduction.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, fake local offices, fake partnerships and invented case studies.
- Add real client evidence as soon as available: industry, initial problem, system scope, implementation period and measurable result.
- Do not use `meta keywords` as a ranking tactic; the keyword lists in code are an editorial map, while rankings depend on visible useful content, links and authority.
